What we do

Why Checklist-Only Governance Fails

Traditional auditors read static checklists and walk away. They don't understand defense supply chain operations, cloud enclave architecture, or machine learning telemetry. Sentinel Paradise designs, builds, and verifies your actual secure data boundaries, endpoint controls, and AI workflows—keeping your operations compliant and your federal contracts audit-ready.

🛡️

Defense Enclave Engineering

CMMC Level 2 · NIST SP 800-171 · DFARS

We build lean, isolated Program Enclaves using PreVeil and FutureFeed to protect Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)—meeting federal mandates without exposing your broader commercial business to audit scope.

🧠

ISO 42001 AI Implementation

AI Governance (AIMS) · CUI Safeguards · HITL Workflows

Commercial AI tools introduce severe data-leakage risks. We deploy your complete AI Management System, enforcing strict Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) rules and Acceptable Use Policies to keep operational innovation fast and CUI safe.

ISO 42001 AI Audits & Pre-Assessments

Telemetry Verification · Data Hygiene · Gap Analysis

We execute rigorous internal audits, data telemetry checks, and risk evaluations to verify that your AI workflows, training datasets, and automated safeguards satisfy ISO/IEC standards before formal certification.
